# Machine Learning Of Salary Prediction Using Random Forest

Data : https://drive.google.com/file/d/1F0eScJlePaZkQJxBNCJHhU6p3pE7LHFO/view?usp=sharing

Introduction of data :
A person's salary is generally influenced by work experience, where the longer one works, the higher the salary. At the beginning of a career (0-5 years), salaries tend to be low but increase rapidly as skills and experience increase. In the middle stage (5-15 years), salary increases still occur, but the rate of growth begins to slow down. After more than 15 years of experience, salaries tend to stabilize or experience smaller increases, depending on factors such as promotions, additional skills, and the industry of work. In addition to experience, other factors such as education, work location, and specialized skills also affect one's salary level.
